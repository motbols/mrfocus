Privacy Policy — Mr. Focus Autopilot

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Mr. Focus Autopilot is a private, single-operator tool. It publishes first-party, AI-assisted video content (men's style and lifestyle tips) to the operator's own social media accounts — including Pinterest, X, TikTok, YouTube and Instagram — using each platform's official API.

What data is used

(1) OAuth access tokens for the operator's own accounts, used only to publish the operator's content and read that same account's boards, posts and post statistics; (2) media files created by the operator for posting.

What is not collected

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data belonging to any third party or member of the public. It has no public sign-up and does not read other users' pins, posts or boards.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on the operator's own computer and are only sent to the issuing platform's official API. The operator can revoke access at any time from the platform's settings, after which stored tokens are deleted.
